Everything a whiteboard should be

Quick to sketch.
Clear to share.

Click to drop a box, drag to connect. The structure of the conversation shows up as you talk.

Shapes that fit the idea

Rectangles, circles, and diamonds. Click empty canvas for a rectangle. Drag a handle, or type an exact size.

Connections that route

Link shapes from either side. Choose tidy elbow lines or straight point-to-point lines for the whole diagram.

Lines and freehand

Draw straight lines with an optional arrow, or sketch freely. Pick a color and thickness. Lines stay easy to click at any zoom.

Notes on anything

Pin a draggable note card to a shape, connection, or line. A dotted line ties each note to its owner.

Auto-arrange

One click lays shapes out in layers from left to right, following who depends on what.

Many files, one window

Open several diagrams at once, each in its own tab. A dot shows which ones have unexported changes.

New in this release

Tidy up in bulk.
Share in one click.

Select many items at once to clean up a busy board. Then save the whole diagram as a crisp picture for slides, chat, or docs.

Select many

Grab everything you need.

Shapes, connections, lines, and drawings select together, so a messy corner of the board is one step to clear.

  • Shift, Ctrl, or Cmd-click an item to add it. Click it again to remove it.
  • Shift-drag a box on empty canvas to add every item the box touches.
  • A connection joins in when the box covers its middle, or when both of its shapes are selected.
  • The side panel counts the selection. Press Delete to remove them all at once.
Export as picture

The whole diagram, as a PNG.

Click the picture button in the toolbar and confirm the file name. Drop the image straight into slides, chat, or a document.

  • Saves the whole diagram, not just the part on screen. Pan, zoom, and selection don’t change it.
  • Shapes, labels, connections, lines, and drawings, in your current line style.
  • Open notes come along with their dotted lines. Closed notes stay out.
  • Twice the canvas resolution, cropped with a small margin. Up to 8000 pixels on the longest side.
Control the details

Keep the board
working for you.

Small touches that keep a live discussion moving instead of fighting the tool.

01

Resize with care

Drag a square handle, and hold Shift on a corner to keep proportions. Circles become ellipses.

02

Snap your angles

Hold Shift while drawing a line to lock it to 0°, 45°, or 90°.

03

Rename in place

Double-click a shape or connection to edit its label.

04

Pan and zoom

Drag empty canvas to move around, scroll to zoom, or use the zoom controls.

05

File names that follow

Each tab shows its file name, and every export proposes the same name. Change it before you save.

06

Start fresh

Reset empties the current file and its view, after you confirm. Other open files stay as they are.

07

Import many at once

Choose several JSON files together. Each one opens in its own tab.

08

Escape hatch

Press Escape to finish drawing, cancel a connection, or clear the selection.

09

Script it

Every action is available from window.DiagramAPI: add shapes, connect, arrange, select many, and export.

Take it with you

Three ways
out of the studio.

Every export asks for a file name first. Pick the format that suits where the diagram is going next.

.png

Picture

A sharp image of the whole diagram, with open notes. Ready to paste anywhere.

.pptx

PowerPoint

Native, editable shapes and lines, scaled to fit one slide. Notes are not included.

.json

Diagram file

Everything you drew, notes included. Import it later to carry on where you left off.

How it works.

The handful of moves you need to run a whole discussion.

ActionHow
Add a shapeUse the toolbar buttons, or click empty canvas for a rectangle.
Connect two shapesClick a port icon on one shape, then a port icon on another.
Draw a line or freehandPick the Line or Draw tool, then drag. Press Escape when you’re done.
Add a noteClick the note icon on a shape or connection. For a line, select it first.
